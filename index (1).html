About us

A growth and learning community for young Africans.

Powered by FUSE Varsity, TrybebyFUSE is a community for individuals looking to build career growth, get access to skills education, opportunities, and support.

In a world overflowing with information but starved of guidance, finding a space that nurtures your curiosity, hones your skills, and connects you to like-minded people is rare. That's exactly what TrybebyFUSE stands for — a community where learning meets action, and ambition meets opportunity.

Here, growth isn't theoretical. It's tangible, measurable, and shared with a Trybe that genuinely cares. Through our collaboration with FUSE Varsity, we provide accessible courses, career-focused workshops, and opportunities to upskill in tech, business, and creative industries — but learning isn't just about content, it's about support.

KITI · For primary school children

Kids in Tech Initiative (KITI)

Powered by TrybebyFUSE, KITI brings basic tech education and awareness directly into schools — planting seeds for the next generation of creators, innovators and problem-solvers.

KITI is an impact-driven program specifically designed for primary school students in underserved areas. To date, it has empowered over 1,100 children across Lagos and Kwara state, introducing them to opportunities in tech through early education and awareness schemes.

This initiative addresses the "resource overload" often found in the tech space by providing a structured, foundational path for young children to begin their digital journey — one clear starting point instead of an overwhelming pile of options. By introducing children to coding, digital literacy and tech opportunities early, in partnership with KAAF, TrybebyFUSE is shaping a pipeline of digital-ready, future-focused leaders who will carry the torch of African innovation.

1

Who it's for

Primary school pupils in underserved communities.

2

What it delivers

Early tech education, coding and digital literacy awareness, and learning packs delivered in-school.

3

Where it runs

Lagos and Kwara state, with KITI 2.0 covering 10 primary schools across both states, in partnership with KAAF and Fuse Varsity.

Mentorship · For early-career members

TrybebyFUSE Mentorship Initiative

Skills are only one side of the equation — the other is mentorship and guidance from people who've walked the path before you.

The TrybebyFUSE Mentorship Initiative connects community members with seasoned experts who provide consistent, actionable guidance. It's designed to fulfil one of our community's core goals: empowering individuals by providing them with guidance and support, whether they're new to a career path or seeking professional advice.

This is more than a check-in system — it's a tracked, structured journey that ensures mentees receive meaningful insights and support, connecting them with professionals in their desired fields. Through this initiative, we've guided over 500 career enthusiasts through structured support and guidance. These aren't just numbers; they represent people whose career paths have been clarified, confidence boosted, and opportunities unlocked.

1

Who it's for

Members new to a career path, or seeking direction and professional advice.

2

What it delivers

A tracked, structured mentorship journey with one-to-one guidance from experienced professionals.

3

Why it matters

Bridging the gap between aspirations and achievements — over 500 career enthusiasts guided so far.

TTSF · For anyone ready to skill up

TrybebyFUSE Scholarship Fund (TTSF)

Bridging the gap between ambition and opportunity, particularly in the tech industry.

The TrybebyFUSE Scholarship Fund (TTSF) ensures that financial barriers don't stop passionate learners from accessing high-quality courses. Over the years, this program has enabled numerous students to build skills that propel them into meaningful careers, in collaboration with FUSE Varsity — with over 500 scholarships disbursed so far.

This initiative seeks to empower people who are hungry for knowledge and passionate about learning and skill development, ensuring that financial constraints do not hinder their growth and potential. By removing obstacles to education, we transform ambition into action. We're currently taking applications for the 6th edition.

1

Who it's for

Anyone eager to acquire new skills or move into a different career path.

2

What it delivers

Financial support toward high-quality courses and skill development, particularly in tech.

3

Why it matters

So a lack of funds never has to be the reason someone's potential goes untapped.
